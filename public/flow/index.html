Focus

A timer you barely have to touch.

Everything happens on the clock itself. No menus in the way while you work.

Gestures, not buttons

Tap to start or pause. Swipe sideways to skip, up for one more minute, down to stop. On Windows: Space, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel.

A ring that shows the time

The ring fills as the session runs and changes colour with the mode: tomato red for focus, green for a short break, blue for a long one.

Long breaks on schedule

Choose how many focus sessions come before a long break. Dots under the clock show how close you are.

Stay in the flow

Start breaks and work sessions automatically, and get a notification when a session ends — even with the app in the background.

Atmosphere

Music and scenes that keep you there.

Twenty focus tracks play while you work, shuffled without repeats. Mark your favourites and they play first.

  • Auto background changes with the time of day: sunrise, day, sunset and a starry night.
  • Or pick Tomato, Forest, Night, Sunrise, Day, Sunset — or your own photo, with adjustable dimming.
  • Mute or skip a track with one tap, without leaving the timer.

Statistics

See a whole year of focus.

Every finished session is counted, so progress is something you can see rather than guess.

Year heatmap

One square per day for the last year, darker on the days you focused longest.

History

Time or sessions per day, week or month, and your most productive weekdays and hours.

Colour labels

Tag sessions by subject or project and see exactly where your time went.

Your data, your file

Export and import backups, or export every session to CSV for a spreadsheet.

Windows

A glass widget that stays out of the way.

On Windows, Flow is a small rounded, translucent window you can pin to any corner of the screen and keep on top while you work.

  • Pin it to a corner or drag it anywhere; it remembers where you left it.
  • Light on your PC: about 0.1% CPU while idle and around 1% with a session running.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Space to start or pause, → to skip, ↑ for one more minute, ↓ to stop.
