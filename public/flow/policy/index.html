Introduction

Flow is a Pomodoro focus timer. It times work and break sessions, plays built-in focus music, shows statistics about your sessions and can remind you to start working. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your device. Your sessions, labels, settings and any background photo you choose are stored only in the App's private storage.
  • The App sends no data. It makes no network requests of its own: no server, no sync, no cloud.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What the App Stores

The App keeps the following, all of it in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your finished focus sessions: when they ended, how long they lasted and their label.
  • Your labels and their colours, and any custom time profiles.
  • Your settings: durations, language, theme, background, notification and reminder choices, and favourite music tracks.
  • A copy of the background photo you pick, if you choose one, and on Windows the licence key you enter.

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information, and it does not back your data up to any cloud.

Permissions

On Android the App asks to post notifications (to tell you when a session ends and for the reminders you set), to schedule exact alarms (so that alert is on time), to vibrate, to restore reminders after a restart and to keep the device awake briefly when an alert fires.

It declares internet access because its interface runs in the system web view and Google Play Billing needs a connection; the App itself sends no data.

It requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone or calendar access. Photos and files are opened only through the system picker, and only the file you pick.

Purchases

The timer, music, backgrounds and statistics are free. Flow Pro is a single, optional purchase.

On Android, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your card or account details; the App only learns whether Pro has been purchased. Google's Privacy Policy applies to that transaction.

On Windows, Pro is unlocked with a licence key that is checked on your computer, without contacting any server, and stored locally.

Backups, Data Storage and Deletion

Backups and CSV exports are written only to a place you choose, and only when you ask. You can delete any session or label inside the App.

Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored. Files you exported are then yours to manage.

Security

Because nothing leaves your device, the security of your data depends on the security of the device itself: its screen lock, encryption and updates.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. It contains an optional in-app purchase.

Your Rights

As the App holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. All of your data is under your control on your device.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
