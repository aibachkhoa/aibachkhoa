Tracy

Android · Evidence board

Snap the evidence. Thread the clues.

Tracy turns your phone into an evidence board. Shoot a photo and it is pinned straight to the board, with the time, place and a fingerprint of the picture locked in. Then link photos and notes with named threads until the story makes sense.

Board

See how it all connects.

One board per case. Photos and notes are pinned like on a wall, and every link between them has a name.

Shoot and post

A square, Polaroid-style frame with 1×, 2× and 3× zoom. The photo lands on the board the moment you take it - no filters, no editing.

Drag to link

Hold a photo or note and drag a thread to another. Name the relation: same place, same person, happened before, contradicts, evidence for…

Sticky notes

Timelines, open questions and theories sit right next to the photos they are about.

Find anything

Pan and pinch-zoom the board, and filter it by text, tag or place to see only what matters right now.

Proof

Every photo stays exactly as taken.

The original data is written the moment you shoot and can never be edited afterwards. What you add on top - title, notes, tags, place - is kept separately.

  • Capture time and GPS location recorded automatically.
  • A SHA-256 fingerprint of the image, so anyone can check it was not altered.
  • Export the whole board as a password-protected PDF (AES-256) to open on any other device.

Great for

Whenever you need to work out what happened.

Home and car insurance claims · rental move-in and move-out records · lost and found · DIY repairs · field research · journalism notes · mystery and escape-room games.
