Introduction

Tracy is an evidence-board app. It takes photos, records when and where each one was taken together with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the image, and lets you pin photos and notes to a board and link them. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your device. Your boards, photos, locations, notes and settings are stored only in the App's private storage.
  • The App sends no data. It has no server, no sync and no cloud backup. Nothing leaves your device unless you export a PDF and choose where to send it.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What the App Stores

The App keeps the following, all of it in its own private storage on your device:

  • The photos you take with the App's camera.
  • For each photo: its capture time, its GPS location (if you allowed location access) and its SHA-256 fingerprint. These are recorded at capture and cannot be changed.
  • What you add yourself: titles, trace notes, tags, place names, sticky notes and the links and labels between items.
  • Your boards and your settings, such as language and display choices.

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information, and it does not back your data up to any cloud.

Permissions

Camera - to take photos inside the App. Photos are saved only in the App's storage, not in your gallery.

Location (precise and approximate) - optional, used only to record where a photo was taken at the moment you take it. The App does not track your location in the background. If you deny it, photos are taken without a location.

Internet - declared because the interface runs in the system web view and Google Play Billing needs a connection; the App itself sends no data.

It requests no contacts, microphone, calendar or storage access.

Exporting and Sharing

You can export a board as a PDF protected with a password you choose (AES-256 encryption). The PDF is created on your device. If you then share it, the App hands it to the Android share sheet and the app you pick (email, messaging, file storage…) handles it under its own privacy policy. We never receive a copy.

Subscription and Purchases

The App is free with limits. Tracy Pro is an optional, auto-renewing monthly subscription with a free trial for new subscribers.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your card or account details; the App only learns whether your Tracy Pro subscription is active. Google's Privacy Policy applies to that transaction. Prices, renewal, cancellation and refunds are described in our terms of sale.

Data Storage and Deletion

You can delete any photo, note, link or whole board inside the App, and it is removed from your device.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes everything it stored. PDFs you exported are then yours to manage.

Security

Because nothing leaves your device, the security of your data depends on the security of the device itself: its screen lock, encryption and updates. Choose a strong password when you export a PDF; we cannot recover it for you.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. It contains an optional subscription.

Your Rights

As the App holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. All of your data is under your control on your device.
