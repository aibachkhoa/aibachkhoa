Subscriptions and free trials

  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (for example monthly or yearly) at the price shown at purchase, until you cancel.
  • Payment is charged to your Google Play or Apple account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of a free trial.
  • A free trial is offered to new subscribers only, once per account. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal: on Android in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • If the price changes we, or the store, will tell you in advance, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

Credit packs and quotas

  • Some apps sell consumable packs — for example minutes of transcription, generation credits or extra quota. The amount in each pack is shown before you buy.
  • Credits are added to your account in that app as soon as the store confirms payment, and are used up as you use the feature. Used credits cannot be returned.
  • Credits have no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and unless the app states an expiry date they do not expire while the app remains available.
  • Quotas included in a subscription reset at the start of each billing period; unused quota does not carry over unless the app says so.

One-time upgrades and licence keys

  • One-time upgrades (for example "Pro") are bought once and stay unlocked for as long as the app is available, on devices using the same store account. Use "Restore purchases" in the app after reinstalling.
  • Desktop versions of some apps are unlocked with a licence key sent to the email address you give at purchase. A key is for one person; the number of computers it can be used on is stated at purchase. Keep your key safe — we can resend it to the purchase email on request.

Prices and taxes

Prices are shown in the store in your local currency before you confirm, and include the taxes the store is required to collect. Purchases on Google Play and the App Store are processed by Google or Apple, which act as the merchant of record for the payment; we never receive your card or bank details.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Google Play: you can request a refund from Google Play within 48 hours of purchase. After that, contact us at contact@aibachkhoa.com with your order number (it starts with "GPA.") and we will review it; we can refund Google Play orders ourselves.
  • App Store: refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Licence keys bought from us directly: email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund; the key is then deactivated.
  • If a paid feature does not work as described and we cannot fix it, you are entitled to a refund whatever the time since purchase.
  • Partial refunds for the unused part of a subscription period are not provided, except where the law requires it.

Right of withdrawal (EU/UK)

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content. Because paid features are supplied immediately, at purchase you are asked to agree that supply begins at once and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once it has begun. This does not affect your rights if a feature is faulty or not as described.

Availability and changes

We work to keep paid features available and working. We may change or retire features over time; if we remove a paid feature you are still paying for, we will give notice and refund any period you have paid for and can no longer use. If an app is withdrawn, we will give at least 30 days' notice in the app where possible.

Support and complaints

Email contact@aibachkhoa.com with the app name and, for purchases, the order number. We reply within 3 working days. EU consumers can also use the EU Online Dispute Resolution platform.
