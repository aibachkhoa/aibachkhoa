Jump Master

Android · pixel-art climbing game

One jump at a time. All the way up.

Jump Master is a calm but demanding pixel-art climber made for touch screens. No running, no double jump — only one perfectly judged leap after another, up ten towering climbs with original music and quiet, muted pixel art.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy
Every ledge is one good jump away. Every fall is your own fault — and the climb waits patiently for you to try again.

How it plays

Hold, aim, let go.

Touch anywhere on the screen — there is no fixed jump button. Two move buttons at the bottom walk you to the edge, and your finger does the rest.

✋

Hold to charge

Touch and hold anywhere. The longer you hold, the higher and further the jump will carry you.

↔️

Slide to aim

Slide the same finger left or right. An arrow under your finger shows exactly where the jump is heading.

🦘

Lift to leap

Let go and you are committed. Land on the ledge — or fall back down and learn the gap.

Climbs

Ten climbs, harder the higher you go.

Each climb is a tower of its own. Further up, platforms get narrower, gaps get wider and the spikes come more often.

Clockwork Spire, Gear Works, Iron Summit

Conveyor belts keep moving under your feet and push you towards the walls. Time the take-off before the belt decides for you.

Crystal Caverns, Frozen Depths, Shard Abyss

Slippery ice. You keep sliding after you land, so a jump that looks perfect can still carry you over the edge.

Sky Citadel, Storm Peaks, Thunder Crown

Clouds you can jump up through, bounce pads that throw you higher, and gusts of wind that bend every jump.

Ancient Ruins

The final challenge: a huge hand-built world of temples, trees and jungle, climbed from the forest floor to the very top.

Checkpoint flags

Flags light up as you touch them. Tap a lit flag at any time to warp straight back to it after a long fall.

Three crowns per climb

One for reaching the summit, one for beating the par time, one for falling as little as possible. Your fastest runs go on a personal ranking board.
