Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Jump Master collects nothing and sends nothing. The game does not use the network, has no account and no advertising, and everything it remembers stays in the app's own private storage on your device.

Information we collect

None. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDK that collects data, no crash reporter and no telemetry of any kind. We never see that you installed the game, never see how you play it, and have no way to identify you.

What is stored on your device

A small amount of data, written by the game into its private storage and readable only by it:

  • Progress — which climbs you have unlocked, where you are on each climb, the checkpoint flags you have lit and the crowns you have earned.
  • Best times and ranking — your fastest runs on each climb, shown on the in-game ranking board, together with the player name you type in the game. The ranking is personal: it is never uploaded or shared, and the name can be anything you like.
  • Settings — whether music and sound effects are switched on.

None of this ever leaves the device. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for short haptic feedback during play. No camera, no microphone, no photos, no location, no contacts, no notifications and no network.

What the app store sees

Downloading and updating the game happens through Google Play, which handles that transaction under its own privacy policy and may show us aggregate, anonymous figures — how many people installed the game in a country, for instance. We receive no personal information from it and cannot connect any of it to a person.

Children

Jump Master is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children. It contains no violence beyond a fall back down the climb. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data about children either. It has no chat, no user-generated content shared with others, no links out and no purchases.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever gains a feature that touches the network, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
