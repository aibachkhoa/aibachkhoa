Treasure Hunters

Android · pixel-art platformer

Grab your cutlass. The treasure won't wait.

Treasure Hunters is a pixel-art pirate platformer made for touch screens. Run, double-jump and slash across beaches, misty valleys and haunted shipwrecks — fifty levels, ten bosses, and a chest of gold at the end of every island.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy
Every island ends with a chest. Every fifth one has something guarding it — and every tenth, that something flies.

How it plays

Short levels, made for one thumb on each side.

Move on the left, jump and slash on the right. A level takes a couple of minutes; a checkpoint flag in the middle means a slip never costs you much.

🏝️

Run, jump, slash

Double-jump over spike pits and rivers, cut through crabs, sharp-toothed fish and starfish, smash barrels and crates for coins.

💀

Three golden skulls

Every regular level hides three. Some sit on the path; some need a perfect double jump off a plank you almost missed.

🪙

Open the chest

Reach the treasure chest to clear the level: the coins burst out and fly straight into your pockets.

Bosses

A boss every fifth level.

Levels 5, 15, 25, 35 and 45 end in a mini-boss arena. Levels 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 bring a ghost ship down out of a storm — each one tougher than the last.

Captain Claw

An armoured lobster with an eyepatch. Its claws sweep wide, and when it leaps, the landing sends shockwaves along the sand — jump them.

Shark Brute

A hammerhead with a spiked club and no patience. It charges the moment you are in range.

Urchin Queen

A crowned sea urchin that curls into a spinning ball of spikes and rolls straight at you.

Bone Captain & Octo Baron

A towering skeleton captain with a broad cutlass, then an octopus noble with a cutlass in every tentacle. At half health, every boss gets angry — and faster.

Five ghost ships

They fire cannon volleys, rain swords on the deck and roll cannonballs along it. Slash a cannonball back into the hull, or wait for the ship to swoop low and strike it.

The Dead Admiral

Level 50. The last flagship, with the most hull, the most cannons and the shortest pauses between them.
