Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Treasure Hunters collects nothing and sends nothing. The game does not request internet access, has no account and no advertising, and everything it remembers stays in the app's own storage on your device.

Information we collect

None. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporter and no telemetry of any kind. We never see that you installed the game, never see how you play it, and have no way to identify you.

What is stored on your device

One small save file, written by the game and readable only by it:

  • Progress — which levels you have unlocked, and your best number of golden skulls and coins in each level.
  • Settings — whether music and sound effects are switched on.

The file never leaves the device and is not included in cloud backups. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for short haptic feedback when you take a hit or clear a level. No camera, no microphone, no photos, no location, no contacts, no notifications and no network.

What the app store sees

Downloading and updating the game happens through Google Play, which handles that transaction under its own privacy policy and may show us aggregate, anonymous figures — how many people installed the game in a country, for instance. We receive no personal information from it and cannot connect any of it to a person.

Children

The game contains mild cartoon fantasy violence — a pirate's cutlass against crabs, skeletons and ghost ships — and no blood. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data about children either. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no links out and no purchases.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever gains a feature that touches the network, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
