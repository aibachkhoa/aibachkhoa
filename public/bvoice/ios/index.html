Meetings

The words arrive while people talk.

Press record and put the phone on the table. Each sentence appears as it is spoken, the newest at the top, so a glance tells you where the conversation has got to.

Text as it is spoken

A line forms while someone is still talking and settles when they pause. You can follow along, or look away and catch up later.

Live translation

Pick a language before the meeting and every line is translated underneath it as it lands — useful when not everyone in the room reads Vietnamese.

Noise in the room, not in the text

Fans, air-conditioning and keyboards are filtered out before the words are written down. The original recording is kept untouched.

Keeps going with the screen off

Lock the iPhone or switch to another app and the meeting carries on recording; the orange indicator shows the microphone is on.

Who said what

Minutes with names on them.

A transcript without speakers is a wall of text. After the meeting BVoice works out how many people spoke and puts a label on every line.

  • Counts the speakers itself. No need to say how many people were in the room.
  • Name each person once. "Linh", "Son", "Minh" — the name then appears on every line they said.
  • Correct anything. Tap a line to fix a word; play the recording back to check it.

Minutes

From recording to a document.

A finished meeting is something you send to people. BVoice gets it there in a couple of taps.

Word and PDF

Export the minutes, with speakers and translations, as a Word document or a PDF — or copy the text and share it to any app.

Translate the whole meeting

Choose any of 100 languages and every line of a past meeting is translated in place.

Find it again

Search your meetings by title or by anything that was said in them.

Listen back

The recording is kept with the transcript, so you can always hear exactly what was said.

Interpreting

Speak Vietnamese. The phone says it in their language.

For hosting guests, guiding a tour or giving a talk. You speak; BVoice translates each sentence and reads it aloud in up to three languages, one after another.

  • English, French, German and Spanish, through the phone's own speaker — no extra device.
  • It waits for your pause. Translations are prepared while you talk and spoken once you stop, so the phone never talks over you.
  • Every sentence on screen, in Vietnamese and in each language, so nobody misses a line.

Permissions

One permission, asked when you record.

iOS asks you first, at the moment you start a meeting — not when the app opens.

  • Microphone. To hear the meeting while you record or interpret. It is off the rest of the time.
  • Audio in the background. So a meeting keeps recording with the screen locked. It applies only while you are recording.
  • Nothing beyond that. No location, no contacts, no camera, no photo library, no tracking.

Privacy

Your meetings stay on your phone.

On iPhone everything happens on the device itself: recognition, speaker labels, translation and speech. No audio, transcript or recording is sent anywhere.

  • No sign-up. Open the app and record.
  • Recordings and minutes are stored only in the app, on this phone. Delete a meeting and it is gone.
  • A one-time download. On first use the app fetches the data it needs to recognise speech — about 35 MB; translation, voices and speaker labels fetch theirs the first time you use them.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 15.1 or later. It runs best on iPhone 12 or newer; older models work, more slowly on long meetings and live translation.

Does my audio leave the phone?

No. On iPhone recognition, translation and speech all run on the device. The only connection the app makes is the one-time download of the data each feature needs, and purchases, which Apple handles.

Which language does it understand?

Vietnamese. Meetings and transcripts can then be translated into 100 languages, and interpreting speaks English, French, German or Spanish.

Do I need an internet connection?

Once, to download the data each feature needs. After that, recording, transcribing, labelling speakers, translating and interpreting all work without a connection.

How accurate is it?

Very good on clear speech, less so with heavy noise, people talking over each other or specialist jargon. Results are produced automatically and can be wrong, so check minutes before relying on them for anything important.

Can I record people without telling them?

Please don't. Tell everyone present that you are recording and ask for their consent, as data-protection law requires in Vietnam and most other places.
