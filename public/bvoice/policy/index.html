Introduction

BVoice records meetings and conversations in Vietnamese, turns them into text, labels who said what, translates them and reads translations aloud. It has no accounts, no user profiles and no social features.

The short version

  • Your recordings and minutes stay on your phone. Recordings, transcripts, translations, speaker names and settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We keep no copy.
  • Processing happens on the phone. Recognition, speaker labelling, translation and speech run on your device. On iPhone, always. On Android, unless the phone is below the minimum hardware — see below.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing in the App measures what you do or follows you across other apps.
  • One permission that matters: the microphone, used only while you record a meeting, interpret, or have the meeting screen open.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card.

What the App Stores

The App keeps the following, all of it in its own private storage on your device:

  • The audio recordings of your meetings.
  • Their transcripts, translations, and the names you give to speakers.
  • Your settings: language, theme, colour, the translation and interpreting languages you chose.
  • Your plan: the trial start date, an active subscription, minutes remaining, and the transaction identifiers of purchases already credited, so a purchase is never counted twice.

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other identifying information. It does not back your data up to any cloud service of ours.

Microphone and Recording

The microphone is used only while the meeting screen is open (to show a sound level; nothing is stored), while you record a meeting, or while you interpret. It is not used at any other time. While recording, Android shows a persistent notification and iPhone shows its orange indicator, so you always know the microphone is on.

You are responsible for telling the people present that you are recording and obtaining their consent, as required by the data protection laws that apply to you — in Vietnam, Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. Do not use the App to record anyone secretly or for any unlawful purpose.

Where Processing Happens

On iPhone, recognition, noise reduction, speaker labelling, translation and speech all run on the device. No audio, transcript or recording leaves it.

On Android, the same is true on any phone that meets the minimum hardware. Only on a phone below that minimum, which cannot transcribe and translate quickly enough by itself, does the App send short audio clips and the sentences to translate to our processing server, over an encrypted connection. They are used only to produce the text or translation and send it back; they are not stored, not used to train anything, and not shared. Full recordings, minutes and speaker names always stay on the phone.

One-Time Downloads

To work on the phone, the App downloads the data each feature needs once — about 35 MB for recognition, more for translation, voices and speaker labelling — from file hosting servers. As with any download, those servers see your device's IP address and ordinary connection details. None of your recordings or other data is sent with the request. Wi-Fi is recommended, as these downloads can use mobile data.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Every feature is free for seven days from the first launch. After that you can subscribe to BVoice Pro, monthly or yearly, or buy packs of transcription minutes.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store, depending on where the App was installed from. We never see, receive or store your card or payment details. The App learns only what you are entitled to: whether a subscription is active, and which purchases to credit.

Cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Permissions and Network Access

On Android the App declares the microphone permission, permission to keep recording in the foreground with a notification, notifications, internet access, and Google Play billing. On iPhone the App asks for the microphone, and declares background audio so a meeting keeps recording while the screen is locked.

The App requests no location, contacts, calendar, camera or photo library access, and no access to your files.

The network is used for the one-time downloads above, for purchases, and on Android phones below the minimum hardware for the processing described above. Recording, transcription, speaker labelling, translation and interpreting otherwise work with no connection.

Data Storage and Deletion

Everything the App stores lives in its own private area on your device. You can delete any meeting from within the App.

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it permanently. Anything you export or share outside the App — a Word file, a PDF, copied text — is then yours to manage.

Accuracy of Results

Transcripts, speaker labels, translations and synthesised speech are produced automatically and may be wrong, incomplete, or attribute words to the wrong person — especially with noise, overlapping speech or specialist terms. Check them before relying on them for anything important, such as official minutes, contracts, medical or legal matters.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It contains in-app purchases, so on a shared device we recommend a purchase PIN on Google Play or Screen Time restrictions on iPhone.

Your Rights

Data protection laws give you rights to access, correct, delete and port the personal data an organisation holds about you. We hold no personal data about you: what exists is on your device, where you can view, edit, export and delete it at any time. For payment data, please contact Google or Apple directly.

Security

Your recordings never leave your device on iPhone, nor on Android phones that meet the minimum hardware, so there is no transfer to intercept and no database of ours to breach. Where Android processing does happen on our server, the connection is encrypted and nothing is kept. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so protect your phone with a passcode.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

When this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a change is material, the App asks you to accept it again before its next download.
